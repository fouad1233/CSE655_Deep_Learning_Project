## How I verified AI output
- Hand calculations vs the code: params, output shapes, MACs and receptive fields of my CNN computed by hand and checked by forward hooks with asserts before training (caught case 11); ResNet18's 11,177,025 params counted layer by layer and checked with `named_parameters()`.
- Measurements over estimates: energy and time come only from CodeCarbon; the AI's estimates were wrong several times (cases 9, 13, 15).
- Sanity checks against known values: the first validation MAE must match the mean baseline (caught case 7); the first-batch loss recomputed by hand (case 10); validation vs test MAE within 0.11 years for every model.
- Looking at the data: galleries per score band (case 2), duplicate galleries, the removed drawing.
- Smoke tests and guards: new code ran on a small subset first; test evaluations are guarded so they cannot run twice; the split is checked for determinism (same MD5).
- Documentation and papers: torchvision weight metadata (case 5), the ResNet paper for the 224 px input, the CodeCarbon documentation.
- Reading the outputs myself: cases 2, 17 and 19 were found because I questioned a figure or a sentence.
- References: all 30 bibliography entries checked against the publishers' records (3 page ranges corrected).
